abstract: |
  CATNIP proposes a retention-data-free objective that combines adaptive reverse-policy calibration with token-level weighting to emphasize high-confidence tokens while limiting collateral forgetting. Without contrastive pairs, the method surpasses GA, NPO, SimNPO, and FLAT on WMDP (Bio/Cyber) and MUSE-Books, remaining robust when unlearning data are scarce or short QA snippets. Ablation studies confirm that both calibration and tokenization components are necessary for stable forget–retain trade-offs.
