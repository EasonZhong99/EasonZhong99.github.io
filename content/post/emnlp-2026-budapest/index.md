I attended the **2026 Conference on Empirical Methods in Natural Language Processing (EMNLP 2026)** in Budapest, Hungary 🇭🇺, where I presented our main-conference paper **"CALIBURN: Self-Calibrated LLM Unlearning Alignment"** at the poster session.

It was great to exchange ideas with so many people in Budapest. The conversations at the poster, on calibration, data-scarce settings, and how to evaluate what a model has really forgotten, taught me a lot, and I came home with plenty of new ideas for my work on post-training and alignment.

Thanks to my co-authors **Zhengbang Yang**, **Dr. [Junyuan Hong](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jyhong836/)**, and my advisor **Dr. [Zhuangdi Zhu](https://zhuangdizhu.github.io/)**, and to everyone who stopped by poster #298!
