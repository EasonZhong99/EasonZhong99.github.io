🎉 I'm delighted to share that our paper **"CALIBURN: Self-Calibrated LLM Unlearning Alignment"** has been accepted to the **EMNLP 2026 Main Conference**.

CALIBURN introduces a **self-calibrated, token-level unlearning objective** that uses the model's *own* confidence to adaptively concentrate forgetting on the high-confidence undesirable tokens. This fine-grained design:

- improves the trade-off between knowledge removal and utility preservation;
- stays effective when unlearning data is scarce;
- and reduces the reliance on retention data and external reference models that most existing methods depend on.
